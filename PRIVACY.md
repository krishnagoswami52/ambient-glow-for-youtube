# Privacy Policy for Ambient Glow for YouTube

Ambient Glow for YouTube does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- Your settings are saved in your browser using Chrome's storage and are never sent to us or to any third party.
- The extension reads video frames only inside your browser, to draw the light effect. Nothing leaves your device.
- There are no analytics, ads or tracking of any kind.
